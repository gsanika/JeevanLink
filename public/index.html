📋 Demo Walkthrough

01
Open all four portals above — ideally on separate screens or browser tabs. Watch the status dots turn green as each node registers.
02
On Phone A (Victim), tap the SOS button and describe the emergency by voice or typing. Hit Send via mesh.
03
Watch Relay B log received → forwarding, then Relay C do the same — each hop takes ~1.4 s to simulate radio delay.
04
The Rescue Dashboard receives the packet sorted by severity. Click Acknowledge to send confirmation back — the ack travels the chain in reverse.
05
Store-and-forward demo: flip Relay C to Out of range before sending, then bring it back. The packet queues at B and auto-delivers when C reconnects.
